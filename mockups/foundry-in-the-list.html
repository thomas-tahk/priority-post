Foundry in the List

Five ways a factory item could sit in your daily list — the last one is the hybrid you asked for. The same items appear in each, so you can compare directly. The frames use the app's real colors, fonts and card styling — this is what it would actually look like.

E is the one

D's density was never the problem — the separate block was. Pulling the strips back into the list keeps the calm one-line read while the factory still has to earn its place next to your real work, and the primary action stays one tap away.

Two things E has to get right, and both are visible above: a long title wraps rather than truncates on a narrow screen, and only one item is open at a time so the list never becomes a wall.

Still open: the Foundry colour. Your seven category colours are locked and taken, so this amber (#b45309) is a new hue, close to learning #ea580c. In E it only appears as the 3px edge, never as a filled pill — so the collision matters much less than it did in A or B.
